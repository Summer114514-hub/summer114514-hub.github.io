Price–volume signals in CSI 300 stock selection, and how much leaderboard advantage survives

manuscript in preparationfirst author · Oct 2026 –

Out of a university competition on CSI 300 index enhancement: 1st of 153 teams on the public leaderboard, 27th of 85 on the private one. The paper asks whether price–volume signals add anything to a LightGBM cross-sectional baseline on a six-year walk-forward (2017–2022), and uses all 85 teams' scores to measure how much public-leaderboard advantage is overfitting.

experiment design and evaluation protocol; an independent script that re-verifies factor RankIC, coverage and the leaderboard regressions; writing. Model code was built with AI tools and checked by me.

Fairness–stability trade-offs in knockout pairings for the 48-team FIFA World Cup

draft · under independent checksole author · 2026

FIFA's Annex C assigns the eight best third-placed teams to group-winner slots through a fixed table over all 495 qualifying-group scenarios. I treat the table as a bi-objective combinatorial mechanism: exposure fairness across source groups versus stability when one qualifier changes. Exact exposure optimum 1/308; a Delsarte linear-programming lower bound on stability loss via the Johnson scheme.

model, proofs and computation. Before submitting, I'm having the two core theorems checked by people outside the project and re-deriving them myself without the draft.
