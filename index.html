Research interests

  1. Financial mathematics

    The mathematics of prices, risk and decisions under uncertainty: stochastic calculus, asset pricing, and the statistics needed to fit any of it to data.

  2. Stochastic control

    Within that, the problem of acting optimally over time when the state evolves randomly: the Merton problem, the HJB equation, and the robust versions where the model itself is uncertain.

  3. Portfolio choice under parameter uncertainty

    Within that, one question I keep returning to: the expected return is the input that matters most and the one the data pin down least. What does "optimal" mean once you take that seriously? I look at it from the data side (estimation error amplified by optimisation) and from the control side (drift uncertainty turns sup into sup–inf, and a no-trade region appears).
